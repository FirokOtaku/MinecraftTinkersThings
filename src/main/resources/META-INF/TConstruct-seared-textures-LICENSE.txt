Tinkers' Construct 1.12.2-2.13.0.183
https://github.com/SlimeKnights/TinkersConstruct/tree/1.12
Upstream authors: boni and KnightMiner (see TConstruct mcmod.info).
The 1.12 branch README licenses code, textures and binaries under MIT.

Tinkers' Things recolored the seared masonry, connected glass, tank and
brick sprites for its ashen, cinder and luminous building materials.
The building-material model and blockstate mappings also adapt the upstream
seared models and mappings. The three stone powder sprites use Tinkers' Things artwork.
